<ParagraphTitle id="2-2-1-the-need-to-check-for-errors">The need to check for errors</ParagraphTitle>

<ParagraphContent id="2-2-1-the-need-to-check-for-errors">

When data is transmitted, there is always a risk that it may be corrupted, lost or even gained. Errors can occur during data transmission due to: interference (all types of cable can suffer from electrical interference, which

can cause data to be corrupted or even lost) problems during packet switching (this can lead to data loss or it is even

possible to gain data!) skewing of data (this occurs during parallel data transmission and can cause

data corruption if the bits arrive out of synchronisation).
